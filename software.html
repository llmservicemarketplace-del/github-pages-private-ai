Three products, and the add-ons that extend them

Two complete Windows applications and a line of specialist assistants. All of them run the AI on your own hardware, with no account and nothing uploaded. Denied claim, contested matter, or a subject you need an expert answer on — the record stays yours.

PAi Claims

Property insurance claim software with a deterministic repair estimator built in. Photographs, carrier estimates, repair costs, correspondence and payment records go into one claim file you control. The software builds the relationships between them and keeps carrier values structurally separate from your own source-traced quantities and prices.

How the two lines differ

PAi products load an AI model onto your own computer during setup and run it there. Once installed they work with no network connection, no account, and no data leaving the machine.

PSi add-ons do their core work locally as well. Where they differ is that they can optionally reach a hosted model through an API when a job needs one larger than a desktop can hold. That path is off until you enable it and supply your own credentials.

The products connect. PAi Claims and PAi Legal share a case, so claim evidence carries into the legal workspace when a denial turns into a dispute.

What everything needs

The software — guided installation, hardware detection, model setup, the workspace, documentation and support.

The AI model — downloaded directly from its publisher under that publisher’s own licence. It is not owned by PAi. You choose which one during setup.

Requirements — Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. 8 GB memory minimum, 16 GB recommended. About 10 GB free disk space. No Python, Docker or developer tools.
