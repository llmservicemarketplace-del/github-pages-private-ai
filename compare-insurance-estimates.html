The practical problem

A total alone does not explain the difference.

Two estimates can reach different totals because they describe different work, use different quantities, apply different prices or handle depreciation and payments differently. PAi Claims helps separate those questions instead of treating the disagreement as one unexplained number.

A flagged difference is not proof that coverage exists or that an insurer must pay it. It is a specific issue to verify against the policy, evidence and appropriate professional guidance.

Questions before downloading

Clear limits and privacy choices.

Can PAi Claims tell me whether the carrier estimate is wrong?

PAi Claims helps compare damage records, repair scope, quantities and pricing. A difference is something to investigate and support. The software does not decide coverage, liability or what an insurer must pay.

Do I have to upload the claim to a PAi cloud account?

PAi Claims keeps case workspaces in ordinary folders on the Windows computer or storage location you choose. If you select an optional external AI provider, information you submit to that provider leaves your device and is handled under its terms.

Does PAi Claims replace a contractor, public adjuster or attorney?

No. PAi Claims is software for organizing, estimating and analyzing claim records. It is not an insurer, public adjuster, law firm or substitute for licensed professional advice.
