Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PAi Luton ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with PAi Legal, a private legal document assistant application available through the Microsoft Store and Microsoft 365.

1. No Personal Data Collected by PAi Legal

PAi Legal is designed to operate entirely on the user's local device. The application does not transmit, collect, store, or share any personal information, case documents, legal files, or user-generated content to PAi Luton or any third party. All document analysis, case records, and legal work product remain on the attorney's machine at all times.

2. Activation Codes

When a user activates PAi Legal using a purchase code, the application sends only the code string to our Cloudflare-hosted verification service to confirm validity. No personal information, device identifiers, or document content is included in this request. The code is not stored on the device after activation.

3. No Analytics or Telemetry

PAi Legal does not collect usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or behavioral data of any kind. We have no visibility into how the application is used once it is installed.

4. No Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user information with third parties. PAi Legal does not integrate advertising networks, social media trackers, or analytics services.

5. Microsoft 365 Copilot Integration

When PAi Legal is used through the Microsoft 365 Copilot integration, workflow coordination metadata (such as case identifiers and encryption tier selection) may be passed to our Cloudflare Worker API. This metadata does not include document content, personal information, or privileged legal material. Microsoft's own privacy policy governs data processed through Microsoft 365 services.

6. Children's Privacy

PAi Legal is intended for use by legal professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of PAi Legal after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
