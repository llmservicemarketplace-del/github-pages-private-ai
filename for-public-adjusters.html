What it does on a file

Independent estimateBuilt from your photos, measurements and the policy, never from the carrier's figures. Unit prices come from real litigation data and regional market prices.
Side-by-side comparisonLoad the carrier's estimate into the case and compare it with PAi's. The gaps show which lines to push on.
Evidence behind every itemPhotos, measurements, documents and correspondence stay linked to the items they support, so a rebuttal starts from one file.
Unconfirmed stays unconfirmedAnything not yet verified is marked that way before the demand goes out, so the carrier doesn't find the weak spot first.
Demand letterDrafted from the confirmed facts in the same case record.
Litigation handoffIf a file goes to suit, PAi Legal opens the same record without re-uploading, and each item keeps its verification status.

Use it alongside Xactimate

Carriers expect estimates in the format they price-check, and you probably write yours in Xactimate already. PAi Claims doesn't ask you to change that. Use it as a second, independent number and as the place where the support for each disputed line lives. When the carrier trims a line, the photos, measurements and documents behind it are already together.

Private by design

Client files stay in a folder you choose on your own Windows PC. AI is optional: run a local model, connect your own API key for a frontier model, or work with no AI at all. AI drafts and summarizes on top of the evidence; it does not decide what is true.

What it is not

PAi Claims is documentation and estimating software. It is not an insurer, a public adjuster, a law firm or a replacement for your license and judgment. Check every line before you send it.
