# Public-records capitol image installation

Installed November 11, 2026 on the 50 state public-records guides and the District of Columbia guide.

`installed-page-image-map.csv` is the authoritative record of the image path, visible alternative text, caption description, creator, license, source URL, and attribution requirement used on each published page.

The original `page-image-map.csv`, `image-rights-register.csv`, `IMAGE-SOURCES.md`, and contact sheet are retained as source-package provenance. Their suggested captions and alternative text were not copied blindly. The published pages use corrected descriptions for archival photographs, architectural details, flags, plaques, monuments, and capitol grounds. Generic claims that every jurisdiction keeps court and property records in county offices were omitted because record custodians vary by jurisdiction and record type.

All Creative Commons images include linked creator, license, and Wikimedia Commons attribution in the visible figure caption. Public-domain and CC0 images include a visible link to their source record.
