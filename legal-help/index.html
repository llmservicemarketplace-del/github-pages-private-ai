Legal Help and Case Preparation Resources

The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. publishes plain-language, educational resources to help individuals, families, and community members navigate civil legal questions and prepare their records effectively.

Facing a legal dispute, property transfer, probate administration, or workplace conflict can be challenging. When individuals first encounter legal questions, they are often confronted with unfamiliar procedural requirements, specialized terminology, and strict filing deadlines. Our educational guides explain these topics in straightforward terms so that you can understand the basics, identify qualified help, and organize your supporting records before speaking with an attorney or legal aid provider.

Organizing Documents and Facts Before Seeking Counsel

Organized records can help make legal consultations more focused and efficient by making important facts, dates, documents, and questions easier to review. Gathering your materials early can help prevent lost time, clarify the core timeline of events, and assist legal counsel in evaluating your matter.

Recommended Record Preparation Checklist:

  • Chronological Summary: A factual, concise timeline detailing what happened, in date order, focusing on documented events.
  • Primary Documents: Signed contracts, deeds, leases, wills, written notices, official letters, and court-stamped papers.
  • Communications: Saved email threads, text message exports, letters, and contemporaneous notes of phone calls or in-person meetings.
  • Financial Records: Invoices, payment receipts, bank statements, estimates, cancelled checks, and relevant tax filings.
  • List of Key Parties: Names, titles, organizations, and current contact details for individuals directly involved in the matter.

Authoritative Directories and Public Legal Services

Free or low-cost civil legal assistance is not available in every location or for every category of dispute. To locate licensed public legal aid programs, legal aid bureaus, and bar referral services in your region, consult established national portals:

  • Legal Services Corporation (LSC) — The largest funder of civil legal aid for low-income Americans, featuring a nationwide directory of local legal aid programs.
  • American Bar Association: Find Legal Help — Comprehensive directory of public service legal clinics, pro bono initiatives, and lawyer referral services.
