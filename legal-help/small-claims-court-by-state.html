Small Claims Court Limits: All 50 States

Last reviewed November 14, 2026. Written by The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit educational resource. General information only, not legal advice. We are not a government agency and we charge nothing for these guides.

Each state sets its own dollar limit for small claims and decides which court hears them. Several states raised their limits in 2024 through 2026. Choose your state for the court, the forms, and the steps.
