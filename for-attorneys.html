What it does

Source-traced factsFacts, statements and allegations are built from the record and stay linked to the page they came from. An allegation stays distinct from proof.
One matter, one folderEach case lives in a folder or drive you choose. Nothing from one client's matter mixes into another's.
Attorney or plain languageAn on-screen switch changes the whole interface between legal terms and plain language, mid-case. Useful for walking a client through their own file.
Evidence gapsThe case map shows how the records relate and what still lacks support.
Document intakeBring in PDFs and scans. Originals are preserved and text is extracted, with OCR for scanned pages.
Insurance disputesProperty claims built in PAi Claims open in PAi Legal from the same record, with each item's verification status kept.

Where the AI fits

The core workspace runs with no AI model. A local model on your machine can phrase findings in prose. For drafting, you can export a brief you choose and send it to a provider with your own API key; that content then follows the provider's terms. The software never lets a model decide what is true in the record.

Confidentiality

Case files stay on your computer, in the location you pick. Nothing is uploaded unless you deliberately use an external AI provider. Review the privacy policy and security notes against your own professional obligations before using it on client matters.

What it is not

PAi Legal is case-organization software. It does not give legal advice, and it does not replace your review of the record or your professional judgment.
