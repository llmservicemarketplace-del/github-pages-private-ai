Last reviewed November 12, 2026. Written by The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit educational resource. General information only, not legal advice. We sell no legal, search, background-check, or filing services.

Court names differ by state, and the right building depends on the type of case and the county. Choose a state for the official directory and a checklist to confirm the address, hours, filing window, accessibility, e-filing, and remote hearings.

Confirm before you travel: courts move and consolidate. Call the clerk or check the court's own page for the address and the last time a filing is accepted.

Common questions

How do I know which court handles my case?

The type of case and the amount in dispute usually decide. Court staff can explain where cases are filed, but they cannot give legal advice. Confirm with the clerk or a legal-aid program.

What if I file in the wrong county?

The court may transfer the case or dismiss it, and you may lose a filing fee or time. Ask the clerk before filing if you are unsure.

Are court addresses online always current?

No. Verify the address and hours on the court's own page or by phone, especially before traveling to a satellite or annex building.
