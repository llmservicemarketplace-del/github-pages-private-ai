Questions about cost

No extra paywall to finish your work.

Does downloading Claims cost $20?

No. Download and setup are free. The $20 paywall appears when you click Start a Claim to create your first claim.

Do I pay again to export or add evidence?

No. Opening, working on, and exporting an existing claim or case does not bring up another PAi paywall.

Is payment a donation?

No. Required software purchases and voluntary donations are separate. You do not need to make a donation to use the software.

Does local AI require API fees?

Local AI processing does not require a hosted AI API. If you choose your own API connection instead, the selected provider’s charges and terms apply.
