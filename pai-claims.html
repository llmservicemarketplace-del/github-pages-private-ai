The problem with a claim spread across twelve folders

A property damage claim generates paper faster than almost anything else in ordinary life. Within a few weeks of a storm, fire or burst pipe you typically have hundreds of photographs, a long carrier estimate, contractor bids, policy pages and email threads that have already forked.

Most people keep this in whatever is closest: photos on the phone, the estimate in Downloads, emails in the inbox, the quote on the kitchen table. What goes missing is the connection between them — which photograph supports which line item, which version the carrier paid on, and what was said on the call that changed the scope.

That connection is exactly what matters if the claim is underpaid, denied or reopened. PAi Claims exists to hold that structure in one place, on hardware you own.

Visible provenance

See where every figure came from. Supported facts stay separate from assumptions and from anything an AI model inferred.

Why carrier values are kept separate from yours

An insurance estimate is one party’s opinion of scope, quantity and unit price. It may be entirely reasonable. It may also miss a room, price a material at a lower grade, or omit labour steps.

The only way to know is to compare it line by line against an independently built quantity takeoff. That comparison falls apart if the two sets of numbers get mixed in a single spreadsheet. PAi Claims keeps the two sets structurally distinct. The carrier’s numbers stay as received. Your own quantities and prices sit alongside, each traceable to the measurement or quote it came from.

Everything stays on your computer

PAi Claims loads its AI model onto your machine during setup and runs it there. After installation the software works with no network connection, no account, and no data leaving the computer. There is no server holding your claim file.

Claim files contain photographs of the inside of your home, your policy number, your address, payment records and correspondence. The safest place for that material is a machine you physically control.

When a claim becomes a legal matter

Denials and underpayments sometimes end in a dispute. PAi Claims and PAi Legal share a case, so claim evidence carries into the legal workspace instead of being rebuilt from scratch. PAi Legal keeps distinct matters separated and can pull authorities from a PSi Legal corpus while the case files stay where they are.

The transition costs nothing extra: PAi Legal is free to download and your first matter there is free.

Common questions

Does PAi Claims send my claim files to a server?

No. The case folder lives on your computer. There is no account to create and no upload step. The software runs with the network disconnected.

Do I need AI to use it?

No. Organizing evidence, comparing estimates and building repair quantities all work without AI. A compatible local model can be added on your own hardware.

Will it tell me what my claim is worth?

No. It organizes the evidence and makes the gap between positions visible. What that gap is worth depends on your policy language, jurisdiction and facts the software cannot see.

Is this a replacement for a public adjuster or attorney?

No. It is an organizational and analytical aid. It does not interpret your policy, give legal advice, or represent you with a carrier.
