How things work in Massachusetts

Program names

Medicaid is MassHealth, SNAP is run by the Department of Transitional Assistance (DTA), and LIHEAP is called fuel assistance.

Small claims up to $7,000

Massachusetts small claims cases, up to $7,000, can be filed in District Court, Boston Municipal Court, or Housing Court. Many evictions go to Housing Court.

Insurance claim problems in Massachusetts

Massachusetts Division of Insurance
Website: www.mass.gov/orgs/division-of-insurance. Consumer line: (617) 521-7777.
The department takes complaints about denied, delayed, or underpaid claims for free.

Questions this guide answers

Who regulates insurance companies in Massachusetts?

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance. It licenses insurers and adjusters and takes consumer complaints for free.

How do I file an insurance complaint in Massachusetts?

Contact the Massachusetts Division of Insurance through its website or at (617) 521-7777. Have your policy number, claim number, the company's letters, and a short timeline of what happened.

Where can I find free legal aid in Massachusetts?

Use LawHelp.org or the Legal Services Corporation's locator and choose Massachusetts, or call 211.
