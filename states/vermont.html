How things work in Vermont

SNAP is 3SquaresVT

Vermont calls SNAP 3SquaresVT. Apply for it and fuel assistance through the DCF MyBenefits portal.

Small claims up to $10,000

Vermont small claims cases, up to $10,000, are filed in the civil division of Superior Court. VTLawHelp has a small claims guide.

Insurance claim problems in Vermont

Vermont Department of Financial Regulation
Website: dfr.vermont.gov. Consumer line: (802) 828-3301.
The department takes complaints about denied, delayed, or underpaid claims for free.

Questions this guide answers

Who regulates insurance companies in Vermont?

The Vermont Department of Financial Regulation. It licenses insurers and adjusters and takes consumer complaints for free.

How do I file an insurance complaint in Vermont?

Contact the Vermont Department of Financial Regulation through its website or at (802) 828-3301. Have your policy number, claim number, the company's letters, and a short timeline of what happened.

Where can I find free legal aid in Vermont?

Use LawHelp.org or the Legal Services Corporation's locator and choose Vermont, or call 211.
