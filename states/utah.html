How things work in Utah

Benefits go through Workforce Services

In Utah, the Department of Workforce Services handles SNAP and Medicaid eligibility, and energy help is the HEAT program. Apply through myCase.

Small claims

Utah small claims are heard in justice court or district court. Check the courts' small claims page for the current dollar limit and forms.

Insurance claim problems in Utah

Utah Insurance Department
Website: insurance.utah.gov. Consumer line: (800) 439-3805.
The department takes complaints about denied, delayed, or underpaid claims for free.

Questions this guide answers

Who regulates insurance companies in Utah?

The Utah Insurance Department. It licenses insurers and adjusters and takes consumer complaints for free.

How do I file an insurance complaint in Utah?

Contact the Utah Insurance Department through its website or at (800) 439-3805. Have your policy number, claim number, the company's letters, and a short timeline of what happened.

Where can I find free legal aid in Utah?

Use LawHelp.org or the Legal Services Corporation's locator and choose Utah, or call 211.
