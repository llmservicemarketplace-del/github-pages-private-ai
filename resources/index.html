Resources for policyholders

Free, independent help for anyone dealing with a property insurance claim: state regulators, case-law databases, disaster aid, legal aid and the records that prove a loss. Every link goes somewhere other than here.

Collected and kept current by NFCcop, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, because the person whose house it is should have the same information the carrier does.
