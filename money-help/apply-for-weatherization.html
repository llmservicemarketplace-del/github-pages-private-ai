How to Apply for Free Home Weatherization

Last reviewed November 8, 2026. General information from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Program rules and amounts change and vary by state; check the official source before you apply.

The federal Weatherization Assistance Program (WAP) pays for energy upgrades that lower heating and cooling bills, at no cost to eligible households. Homeowners and renters can apply. Renters need the landlord's permission.

Who usually qualifies

  • Household income at or below 200% of the federal poverty guidelines, or
  • Someone in the household gets SSI or TANF. Many states also prioritize older adults, people with disabilities, and families with children.

What it may cover

  • An energy audit of your home.
  • Insulation and air sealing.
  • Repair or replacement of unsafe or inefficient heating and cooling systems.
  • Health and safety fixes such as carbon monoxide and ventilation issues.

How to apply

  1. Contact your state weatherization agency or local community action agency.
  2. Apply with proof of income and household size. Applying for LIHEAP at the same agency can speed things up.
  3. Expect a waiting list in many areas. Apply early.

Free local help

Call or text 211, or visit 211.org, to find local programs for bills, food, housing, and more. You can also browse our free resource directory.

Questions this guide answers

Who qualifies for free weatherization?

Generally households at or below 200% of the federal poverty guidelines, or receiving SSI or TANF. States may prioritize older adults, people with disabilities, and families with children.

Can renters get weatherization?

Yes, with the landlord's permission.
