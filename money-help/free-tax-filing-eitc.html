Free Tax Filing and the Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC)

Last reviewed November 8, 2026. General information from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Program rules and amounts change and vary by state; check the official source before you apply.

Many working people can file for free and get money back through refundable credits like the Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC). Paid preparers aren't required.

Free ways to file

  • IRS Free File: free guided software from IRS partners. For the 2026 filing season, it was available at adjusted gross incomes of $89,000 or less.
  • VITA: free in-person help from IRS-certified volunteers, generally for incomes around $67,000 or less, people with disabilities, and limited-English speakers.
  • TCE: free help focused on people 60 and older.
  • Note: IRS Direct File ended and is not available.

Limits change each year. Check IRS.gov before you file.

Earned Income Tax Credit

  • For low- and moderate-income workers, larger with children.
  • For tax year 2026 (filed in 2027), the maximum is $8,231 with three or more qualifying children.
  • It's refundable: you can get it even if you owe no tax.
  • You must file a return to get it, even if you don't otherwise need to.

Watch for refund advance and "rapid refund" fees, which can eat into your refund.

Free local help

Call or text 211, or visit 211.org, to find local programs for bills, food, housing, and more. You can also browse our free resource directory.

Questions this guide answers

Is IRS Direct File still available?

No. IRS Direct File has ended. IRS Free File, VITA, and TCE are free alternatives.

What is the maximum Earned Income Tax Credit for 2026?

For tax year 2026, up to $8,231 for taxpayers with three or more qualifying children, according to the IRS.
