GoodRx Privacy Settlement

A proposed privacy settlement for eligible U.S. users of GoodRx websites, apps, or services. Payment depends on approved claims.

Who may qualify: People in the United States who used a GoodRx website, app, or service before April 20, 2024, subject to the exclusions in the official notice. Read that notice before claiming.

Deadline: December 29, 2026, 11:59 p.m. Eastern online; paper claims must be postmarked by December 29.

Amazon Prime FTC Refunds

Automatic refunds for eligible Prime customers under the FTC settlement. A claim form is not required.

Who may qualify: U.S. Prime customers affected by the specified enrollment or cancellation processes between June 23, 2019 and June 23, 2025, and meeting the usage limits. Review the FTC page for the full conditions.

Deadline: Automatic payments continuing through April 2027; accept payments within 60 days of issue.

Downloads, submission, and updates

A preparation HTML file keeps your details and the next steps in one file. It is not an official claim. Where a reviewed official PDF template is supported, we prefill the contact boxes and leave payment choices, declarations, dates, and signatures for you to complete.

Use the official online claim form, or mail the completed official paper form where accepted. An email button appears only when email submission is specifically verified as accepted. Opening an email draft does not attach your downloaded file; attach it yourself before sending.

The feed checks daily when the scheduled update succeeds. New FTC entries link to the FTC's own instructions. Material changes to a reviewed source pause PDF preparation until the details are reviewed again. Check the official source before you act.
