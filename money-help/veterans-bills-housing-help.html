Help for Veterans Behind on Bills or at Risk of Losing Housing

Last reviewed November 8, 2026. General information from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Program rules and amounts change and vary by state; check the official source before you apply.

Veterans and their families have access to programs on top of general assistance.

If you're at risk of losing housing

  • Call the National Call Center for Homeless Veterans at 877-424-3838, available 24/7.
  • SSVF (Supportive Services for Veteran Families) grantees can help with rent, utility deposits and arrears, moving costs, and case management for eligible veterans who are homeless or at risk.
  • HUD-VASH vouchers combine rental assistance with VA case management.

Other help

  • VA benefits check: make sure you're getting any disability compensation or pension you qualify for.
  • Veterans service organizations and county veterans service officers help with claims for free.
  • Utilities and phone: the Veterans Pension counts for Lifeline eligibility.
  • Food: under 2026 SNAP rules, veterans are no longer automatically exempt from work requirements. See applying for SNAP.

In crisis? Call or text 988 and press 1 for the Veterans Crisis Line.

Free local help

Call or text 211, or visit 211.org, to find local programs for bills, food, housing, and more. You can also browse our free resource directory.

Official and nonprofit sources

  • VA: Homeless programs
  • VA.gov

Need help sorting out bills or applications? Contact NFCcop for free guidance.

Questions this guide answers

Who do veterans call if they're about to lose housing?

The National Call Center for Homeless Veterans at 877-424-3838, available 24/7.

What is SSVF?

Supportive Services for Veteran Families, a VA-funded program that helps eligible veterans who are homeless or at risk with rent, utilities, and case management.
