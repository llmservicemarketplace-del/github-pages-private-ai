How to File a VA Disability Claim

Last reviewed November 15, 2026. General information from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Program rules and amounts change and vary by state; check the official source before you apply.

VA disability compensation is a monthly tax-free payment for veterans with an illness or injury caused or made worse by military service. Filing is free, and free accredited help is available.

File a VA disability claim (official VA site)

Who may qualify

  • You served on active duty, active duty for training, or inactive duty training.
  • You have a current illness or injury that affects your body or mind.
  • The condition is linked to your service: it began in service, was made worse by service, or is caused by another service-connected condition.
  • Your discharge was under conditions other than dishonorable.

How to file

  1. Submit an intent to file. It holds your potential start date for up to a year while you gather evidence.
  2. Gather your evidence. See the list below.
  3. File the claim online at VA.gov, by mail, in person at a VA regional office, or through an accredited representative.
  4. Go to every VA claim exam that is scheduled. Missing one can lead to a denial.
  5. Track the claim in your VA.gov account and answer requests quickly.

Evidence to gather

  • Your DD-214 or other separation papers.
  • Service treatment records.
  • VA and private medical records that show your current diagnosis and treatment.
  • Statements from you, family, or people you served with that describe what happened and how the condition affects you.
  • A medical opinion linking the condition to your service, if you have one.

Free help

Accredited Veterans Service Organization representatives and state or county veterans offices help with claims at no charge. No one may lawfully charge you to prepare or file an initial claim.

If you disagree with the decision

You can ask for a higher-level review, file a supplemental claim with new evidence, or appeal to the Board of Veterans' Appeals. Deadlines apply, generally one year from the decision. For help with bills while you wait, see help for veterans behind on bills.

Free local help

Call or text 211, or visit 211.org, to find local programs for bills, food, housing, and more. You can also browse our free resource directory.

Questions this guide answers

Does it cost money to file a VA disability claim?

No. Filing is free, and accredited Veterans Service Organization representatives help at no charge.

What is an intent to file?

It tells the VA you plan to file a claim and can preserve an earlier effective date while you gather evidence, generally for up to one year.
