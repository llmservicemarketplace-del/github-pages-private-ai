Amazon Prime FTC Refund: Eligibility and Payment Help

Looking for the Amazon Prime settlement refund? Start with the eligibility rules and the official payment instructions below. This is free guidance from NFCcop, The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. We do not administer the settlement or issue refunds.

Quick answer: This program sends automatic payments to eligible customers. It is separate from settlements that accept new claim forms.

Details reviewed November 16, 2026 against the official FTC notice. Check the official page before acting.

Who may qualify for an Amazon Prime refund?

The FTC lists three requirements: U.S. Prime membership; a covered enrollment or unsuccessful cancellation during June 23, 2019–June 23, 2025; and no more than 20 Prime benefits used in a 12-month period after enrollment. Read its definitions and exclusions.

Do I need to file a claim?

No form is required for this automatic-payment program. The FTC says payments continue through April 2027 and expire 60 days after issue.

Where can I get payment help?

The FTC lists SubscriptionMembershipSettlement.com, admin@subscriptionmembershipsettlement.com, and 1-888-999-8094.

A short payment-check checklist

  1. Open the official notice from the link above and compare its rules with your own account history.
  2. Keep any notice and payment reference together, so you can explain a payment question accurately.
  3. Use the contact route on the official notice. Describe what happened and keep a copy of your question and response.

Keep control of your information

NFCcop does not need your Amazon password, bank information, or Social Security number to provide this guide. Do not enter those details here. A promised guaranteed refund in exchange for payment is a warning sign; check the official notice before responding.
