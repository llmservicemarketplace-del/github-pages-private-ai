What Can You Buy With EBT (SNAP Benefits)?

Last reviewed November 15, 2026. General information from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Program rules and amounts change and vary by state; check the official source before you apply.

SNAP benefits on an EBT card pay for food your household eats at home. They do not pay for hot prepared food, alcohol, or non-food items.

See the official SNAP eligible food list (USDA)

What you can buy

  • Fruits and vegetables.
  • Meat, poultry, and fish.
  • Dairy products.
  • Breads and cereals.
  • Snack foods and non-alcoholic drinks.
  • Seeds and plants that produce food for your household.

What you cannot buy

  • Beer, wine, liquor, cigarettes, or tobacco.
  • Vitamins, medicines, and supplements.
  • Hot foods and foods prepared to eat in the store.
  • Pet food, cleaning supplies, paper products, and other household goods.
  • Hygiene items and cosmetics.

Some states have begun restricting certain items, such as soda or candy. Check with your state SNAP agency for the rules where you live.

Where you can use the card

  • Grocery stores, supermarkets, and many convenience stores that display the SNAP or EBT sign.
  • Many farmers markets. Some match your SNAP dollars for fruits and vegetables.
  • Approved online grocery retailers. SNAP can pay for the food but not for delivery or service fees.

Protect your benefits

  • Change your PIN often and never share it.
  • Check your balance and transaction history regularly.
  • Use your state's card lock or freeze feature if it has one.
  • Report a lost or stolen card to your state EBT line right away.

Questions this guide answers

Can I buy hot food with EBT?

Generally no. SNAP does not cover foods that are hot at the point of sale, except in limited programs some states run for certain households.

Can I use EBT to order groceries online?

Yes, at approved retailers. SNAP benefits cannot pay delivery or service fees.
