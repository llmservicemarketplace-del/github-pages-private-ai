Find and organize unclaimed money

Start with an official state search, record possible matches, and prepare a document checklist. Searching and claiming through state programs is free.

Your entries stay in this browser and files you download. This tool does not send your profile, application notes, or letters to NFCcop or an AI service. Remembering data is optional; avoid it on a shared device.
Search first; claim through the state. This tool does not search private databases, verify ownership, decide heirship, or submit claims. Check states where you have lived or worked. Do not upload ID documents, enter a Social Security number, or provide bank details here.
