Help Paying Your Energy Bill in Oklahoma (LIHEAP)

Last reviewed November 14, 2026. Written by The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit educational resource. General information only, not legal advice. We are not a government agency and we charge nothing for these guides.

Short answer: Oklahoma runs the federal Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP). Start here: OKDHS LIHEAP.

If you have a shutoff notice, call your utility today and ask for a payment plan, then apply for crisis assistance.

Energy assistance is a grant that helps pay a heating or cooling bill. It is usually paid straight to the utility or fuel company, and you do not pay it back. This page covers where to apply in Oklahoma, who qualifies, what to have ready, and what else to try.

What the program can pay for

  • Regular bill help: a one-time payment toward heating, and in some states cooling.
  • Crisis help: faster help when you have a shutoff notice, are already disconnected, or are almost out of fuel.
  • Repairs: some states fix or replace an unsafe furnace or air conditioner.
  • Weatherization: insulation and air sealing to lower future bills. See applying for weatherization.

Each state decides which of these it offers and when. Many open for heating in the fall and close when the money runs out.

Who qualifies

Eligibility is based on household income and size. Oklahoma sets its own limit inside a federal range. Households with an older adult, a person with a disability, or a young child are often served first. If you already get SNAP, SSI, or TANF, tell the agency; in many states that makes approval simpler.

Renters can qualify, including when heat is included in the rent in some states. Ask.

What to have ready

  • Photo ID for the person applying
  • Social Security numbers for household members, if the agency asks for them
  • Proof of income for everyone in the home for the period the agency names
  • Your most recent utility or fuel bill, with the account number
  • Any shutoff or disconnection notice
  • Your lease, if utilities are part of your rent

Step by step

  1. Call the utility first if you are behind. Ask for a payment plan and ask what protections apply. See utility payment plans.
  2. Find your local agency. Start at OKDHS LIHEAP.
  3. Apply as early as you can. Funds are limited each year.
  4. Say so if it is an emergency. A shutoff notice, no heat, or a medical need can move you into the crisis track.
  5. Tell the utility you applied. Some will hold a disconnection while an application is pending.
  6. Keep the approval letter. It shows the amount and which bill it was paid to.

If you are facing a shutoff

States limit when a utility can disconnect service, for example during extreme weather or when someone in the home has a serious medical condition. The rules differ by state and by type of utility. See utility shutoff protections and help paying heating bills.

Also ask about water bill help and emergency rent help if more than one bill is behind.

Common questions

How do I get help paying my electric or gas bill in Oklahoma?

Oklahoma runs the federal Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program, known as LIHEAP. Start at the state's official page: OKDHS LIHEAP. You usually apply through a local agency, and funds are limited, so apply early in the season.

What is LIHEAP called in Oklahoma?

Oklahoma runs the federal Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program, known as LIHEAP.

Can I get help if I already have a shutoff notice?

Often, yes. Most state programs have crisis assistance for households facing disconnection or running out of fuel. Tell the agency about the notice when you apply, and call the utility the same day to ask for a payment plan.

Do I have to pay energy assistance back?

No. LIHEAP is a grant, usually paid straight to your utility or fuel company. It is not a loan.
