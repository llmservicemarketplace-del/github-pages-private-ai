Prepare a billing-dispute letter

Explain a duplicate charge, missing refund, subscription problem, or billing error. Review and download a letter you can send yourself.

Your entries stay in this browser and files you download. This tool does not send your profile, application notes, or letters to NFCcop or an AI service. Remembering data is optional; avoid it on a shared device.
Choose the right process. A merchant refund request and a formal credit-card billing-error notice are different. For a credit-card billing error, CFPB says to notify the issuer promptly and send a written notice within 60 calendar days. Use its billing-inquiry address, which may differ from its payment address. Debit-card, bank-transfer, loan, and debt-collection disputes can have different procedures. Do not wait for a merchant reply if an issuer deadline is approaching. Read CFPB instructions.

Tell the company what happened

Only include accurate facts. Add any account information required by the issuer through its verified secure process. This tool stores no full account number and does not establish that a legally sufficient notice has been delivered.

Review your draft

Check names, dates, facts, recipient, and attachments. Go back to edit fields and prepare again if needed. No signature or delivery is completed for you.
