Property Tax Relief After Disaster Damage

Last reviewed November 10, 2026. Free public guidance from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. This is general information, not tax or legal advice. Rules differ by state and change over time, so confirm with the official sources below.

If a disaster damaged or destroyed your home, you may be able to lower your property tax bill or get a refund. The rules depend on your state and when the damage happened compared to your county's assessment date. Deadlines are strict, so contact your county property appraiser or tax assessor early.

Florida: catastrophic event refund

  • If your home was uninhabitable for at least 30 days because of a catastrophic event, you may get a partial refund of that year's property taxes.
  • The refund is based on the drop in value and the number of days the home was uninhabitable.
  • Apply to your county property appraiser on the state form (DR-465) by March 1 of the year after the event, with proof such as utility bills, insurance records, contractor statements, or permits.
  • The appraiser decides by April 1. If denied, you can petition the value adjustment board within 30 days.

Georgia

  • Property is valued as of January 1. Damage after that date usually affects the next year's assessment.
  • When you get your annual assessment notice, you generally have 45 days to appeal if the value doesn't reflect damage.
  • Contact your county tax assessor's office about the damage and ask how it will be reflected.

Other states

Many states have "calamity" or disaster reassessment rules that can lower the value of a damaged home mid-year, often with short filing deadlines. Ask your county assessor what's available.

Other tax help

You may also be able to claim a casualty loss on your federal return or get filing extensions. See IRS disaster tax relief. Keep paying property taxes on time while you apply unless told otherwise; some refunds require timely payment.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a property tax refund if my house was destroyed?

In some states. Florida offers a partial refund if your home was uninhabitable for at least 30 days due to a catastrophic event. Apply by March 1 of the following year.

When do I appeal property taxes in Georgia?

Generally within 45 days of the date on your annual assessment notice.

Who do I contact about disaster property tax relief?

Your county property appraiser or tax assessor's office.
