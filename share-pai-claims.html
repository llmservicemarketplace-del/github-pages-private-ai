Ready-to-share description

A short, accurate blurb for resource lists and newsletters.

PAi Claims is private-first Windows software that helps homeowners organize property-damage photographs, documents, measurements, estimates and correspondence. Download and setup are free; the introductory first claim is $20. Free disaster-recovery guides and assistance links are available at yprivateai.com. PAi Claims is documentation software—not an insurer, public adjuster, law firm or substitute for licensed professional advice.

What a homeowner can do

One resource, four practical steps.

1 · Preserve the original recordsKeep photographs, policies, estimates, receipts, invoices and correspondence without overwriting their source.
2 · Build a supported repair scopeConnect observed damage to measurements, quantities, repair methods, materials, labor and equipment.
3 · Compare separate positionsReview the carrier estimate against the documented damage and independent scope instead of comparing only totals.
4 · Identify the next questionFlag differences that need another photograph, contractor explanation, policy review or licensed professional guidance.
Clear limits: The software does not decide coverage, liability or what an insurer must pay. A difference is a question to investigate and support, not proof of entitlement.
