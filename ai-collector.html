PAi Collector

Private assistant for collectibles, antiques, art and memorabilia — identification, condition and market reasoning.

What you can ask it

  • Help me identify what I am looking at
  • What should I photograph and measure to describe this properly?
  • How do I grade the condition of this honestly?
  • What are the common reproductions and fakes in this category?
  • What provenance would matter for this piece?
  • How should I clean or store this without damaging it?
  • Help me write an accurate listing description
  • What drives value in this collecting area?
  • Is this worth having professionally authenticated?

It runs on your computer, not a server

PAi Collector loads an AI model onto your own Windows PC during setup and runs it there. After installation it works with the network disconnected. Nothing you type is uploaded, logged or used for training, and there is no account and no subscription.

You choose the engine during setup from Qwen, Gemma, Llama, DeepSeek, and PAi checks your memory, disk space and graphics first so it only offers what your machine can actually run. The recommended tier for this assistant is Standard.

How to get it running

  • Buy PAi Collector and receive an activation code.
  • Install PAi from the Microsoft Store.
  • Open it, enter your code, and let it check your hardware.
  • Pick an engine. PAi downloads and configures it for you.

What it will not do

This is not an appraisal and not an authentication. Value depends on condition, provenance and current demand, and this assistant runs offline with no access to recent sales. Get a qualified appraiser or a recognised authentication service involved before you buy, sell or insure anything of significant value.
