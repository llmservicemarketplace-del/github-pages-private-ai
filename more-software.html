PSi Legal

Add-on for PAi Legal — suggests cases to look up, and indexes every case you own

Describe your issue and PSi Legal comes back with the authority worth pulling, from an index of 5,449,347 legal records, each result carrying a source id. It points you at the cases; you read them in the reporter. The same subscription indexes your entire existing library in house — every brief, opinion and prior matter, unlimited, nothing uploaded — into that same search. Rebuilt every year so it does not go stale. PSi Legal is the add-on that goes past the sample. Point it at your own folder of opinions and it builds a full searchable local corpus — no account, no model, no upload — and PAi Legal reads from it as the research shelf.

$30 / month · unlimited in-house indexing · corpus rebuilt yearly · Windows 10/11 · runs entirely offline · no AI required
