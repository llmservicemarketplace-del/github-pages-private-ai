PAi Legal Document Assistant

Private reference assistant for understanding legal documents, terminology and processes. Educational reference only.

What you can ask it

  • Explain what this clause actually means
  • What does this legal term mean in plain English?
  • What questions should I ask a lawyer about this?
  • Help me understand this process step by step
  • What should I look for before signing something?
  • Explain the difference between these two document types
  • Help me organise my notes before a legal appointment
  • What information will a lawyer need from me?
  • Explain what this notice is asking me to do

It runs on your computer, not a server

PAi Legal Document Assistant loads an AI model onto your own Windows PC during setup and runs it there. After installation it works with the network disconnected. Nothing you type is uploaded, logged or used for training, and there is no account and no subscription.

You choose the engine during setup from Qwen, Gemma, Llama, DeepSeek, and PAi checks your memory, disk space and graphics first so it only offers what your machine can actually run. The recommended tier for this assistant is Advanced.

How to get it running

  • Buy PAi Legal Document Assistant and receive an activation code.
  • Install PAi from the Microsoft Store.
  • Open it, enter your code, and let it check your hardware.
  • Pick an engine. PAi downloads and configures it for you.

What it will not do

This is general legal information, not legal advice, and no solicitor-client or attorney-client relationship is created. Law varies enormously by jurisdiction and changes constantly, and this assistant runs offline with no access to current statutes or case law. Always consult a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction before acting on anything legal.
