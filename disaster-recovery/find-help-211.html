How 211 Works: Find Local Help

Last reviewed November 6, 2026. Free public guidance from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. This is general information, not legal or professional advice. Rules and deadlines can change, so confirm with the official sources below.

211 is a free, confidential phone number that connects you with local help for almost any basic need: paying bills, food, housing, shelters, transportation, health care, and disaster recovery. It's run by local organizations like United Ways and community action agencies across the country.

How to reach 211

  • Call 211 from most phones. Calls are confidential and can be anonymous.
  • Search online at 211.org to find your local 211 and its website. Many local 211s also offer chat or text.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 instead.

What 211 can help you find

  • Utility and heating bill help, including LIHEAP. See help paying heating bills.
  • Food: food banks, pantries, and meal programs
  • Housing: rent help, shelters, and warming or cooling centers
  • Disaster recovery: where to get supplies, cleanup help, and assistance after storms
  • Health: clinics, mental health, and substance use services
  • Transportation to medical appointments or shelters

Tips for a faster call

  1. Have your ZIP code ready.
  2. Say exactly what you need, like "help paying my electric bill" or "a warming center tonight."
  3. Mention anything that may qualify you for priority, like being 65 or older, having a disability, having young children, or using medical equipment.
  4. Write down every program name, phone number, and what documents to bring.
  5. Ask what to do if the first program is out of funds.

During and after disasters

After big storms, 211 lines often become the hub for local recovery information: shelters, supply sites, debris pickup, and volunteer cleanup crews. Also check:

  • Finding disaster help near you
  • Applying for FEMA assistance
  • Disaster food assistance

Frequently asked questions

What is 211?

211 is a free, confidential phone number that connects people with local help for bills, food, housing, health care, transportation, and disaster recovery.

Is 211 free?

Calling 211 is free and confidential. Calls can be anonymous.

Can 211 help me pay my electric bill?

211 can connect you with local programs that help pay utility bills, including LIHEAP and charity programs.
