Dialysis Disaster Planning

Last reviewed November 9, 2026. Free public guidance from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. This is general information, not legal or professional advice. Rules and deadlines can change, so confirm with the official sources below.

Missing dialysis can become dangerous within days. Hurricanes, floods, and outages can close dialysis centers and cut off water and power. Planning ahead, and knowing who to call, helps you keep getting treatment.

Before a disaster

  • Ask your dialysis center for its emergency plan, its emergency phone number, and where you'd go if it closes.
  • Keep a card in your wallet with your treatment schedule, prescription (dialyzer, bath, and time), access type, medicines, and your nephrologist's number.
  • Ask your care team for a copy of an emergency kidney diet and keep a 3-day supply of the approved foods. It limits potassium, sodium, phosphorus, and fluids to help you get through if treatment is delayed.
  • If your center offers it, get treatment early before a storm arrives.
  • Home dialysis patients: keep extra supplies, know how to disconnect safely, and register with your utility's medical program. See medical equipment during an outage.

If your center is closed

  • Call your dialysis company's emergency line. Large companies can usually place you at another center, even in another state.
  • Call the KCER (Kidney Community Emergency Response) hotline at 866-901-3773 if you can't reach your center or need help finding treatment.
  • If you evacuate, tell the shelter right away that you're on dialysis. See finding a shelter.
  • Follow the emergency diet strictly until you get treatment.

When to call 911

Call 911 if you have chest pain, trouble breathing, severe swelling, confusion, or a very slow or irregular heartbeat. Missed dialysis can cause dangerous potassium levels and fluid buildup.

Evacuation and transportation

If you don't drive, register with your county's special needs or transportation registry before storm season. See evacuating without a car and disability emergency planning.

Medicines

Bring all medicines and a list of doses when you leave. If they're lost, see replacing medication after a disaster.

Frequently asked questions

What should dialysis patients do if their center closes in a disaster?

Call your dialysis company's emergency line or the KCER hotline at 866-901-3773 to find another center, and follow your emergency kidney diet until treatment.

What is the emergency kidney diet?

A short-term diet from your care team that limits potassium, sodium, phosphorus, and fluids to help you stay safe if dialysis is delayed. Keep a 3-day supply ready.

When should a dialysis patient call 911?

For chest pain, trouble breathing, severe swelling, confusion, or a very slow or irregular heartbeat.
