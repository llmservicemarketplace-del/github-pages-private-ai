FEMA Hotel Assistance (Transitional Sheltering Assistance)

Last reviewed November 10, 2026. Free public guidance from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. This is general information, not legal or professional advice. Rules and deadlines can change, so confirm with the official sources below.

After some disasters, FEMA turns on Transitional Sheltering Assistance (TSA). It pays a participating hotel directly so displaced families have a safe place to stay for a short time. TSA isn't offered in every disaster, so check whether it's active for yours.

Who may qualify

  • You applied for FEMA assistance for a disaster where TSA is active.
  • You can't live in your home because it was damaged, the area is closed, or essential utilities are out.
  • Your housing needs aren't already met by insurance, a shelter, or other help.

There's no separate TSA application. FEMA checks when you apply at DisasterAssistance.gov or call 1-800-621-3362.

What FEMA pays and what you pay

FEMA paysYou pay
Room cost and taxesFood and room service
Non-refundable pet feesLaundry, parking, phone calls
Any incidental deposit the hotel charges at check-in

How to find a hotel

  1. Get your FEMA registration number.
  2. Search participating hotels at femaemergencyhotels.com.
  3. Call the hotel to confirm a room is open before you drive there.
  4. Bring photo ID. Large families can usually get one room per four people, with an adult in each room.

Checkout and how long it lasts

  • FEMA reviews eligibility regularly, for example when your home becomes safe or roads reopen.
  • FEMA usually tells you about 7 days before your eligibility ends, by the contact method you chose.
  • If you don't use TSA for 30 or more days, you may lose eligibility.
  • Start looking for longer-term housing early. See FEMA rental assistance and renters' rights after a disaster.

If TSA isn't active, see how to find an emergency shelter, or ask FEMA about Displacement Assistance.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a FEMA hotel?

Apply for FEMA assistance, then if TSA is active for your disaster, search participating hotels at femaemergencyhotels.com with your registration number and call to confirm a room.

Does FEMA pay for food at the hotel?

No. TSA covers the room, taxes, and non-refundable pet fees. You pay for food, laundry, parking, and other incidentals.

How long can I stay in a FEMA hotel?

It depends on the disaster and your situation. FEMA reviews eligibility regularly and usually notifies you about 7 days before it ends.
