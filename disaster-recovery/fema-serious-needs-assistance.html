FEMA Serious Needs Assistance: The One-Time Payment for Urgent Needs

Last reviewed November 10, 2026. Free public guidance from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. This is general information, not legal or professional advice. Rules and deadlines can change, so confirm with the official sources below.

Serious Needs Assistance is a one-time FEMA payment to help households pay for urgent needs right after a disaster: water, food, first aid, prescriptions, baby formula, diapers, personal hygiene items, and fuel for transportation. It's part of FEMA's Individuals and Households Program.

How much is it?

Disaster declaredPayment per household
On or after October 1, 2026$820
October 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026$790

FEMA adjusts the amount each year for inflation. It's a flat amount for the household, not per person, and you get it once per disaster.

Who may qualify

  • Your area is in a federally declared disaster where FEMA Individual Assistance is available.
  • You apply to FEMA and show you have an urgent need caused by the disaster.
  • Your home was damaged or you were displaced, and FEMA can verify your identity and that you lived there.

Not everyone who applies gets it. Eligibility depends on the disaster and your answers.

How to apply

  1. Apply at DisasterAssistance.gov, in the FEMA app, by calling 1-800-621-3362, or at a Disaster Recovery Center.
  2. Answer honestly that you have urgent needs for things like food, water, or medicine.
  3. Choose direct deposit if you can. It's faster than a mailed check.

What it isn't

  • It's not a loan. You generally won't have to repay it unless it duplicates another payment for the same need.
  • It's separate from other FEMA help, like home repair or rental assistance, which you may also qualify for.
  • It doesn't cover long-term needs. Keep your FEMA application active and respond to letters.

Watch for scams

FEMA never charges a fee to apply, and real FEMA staff carry federal photo ID. See disaster scams. For food help, also see disaster food assistance (D-SNAP).

Frequently asked questions

How much is FEMA Serious Needs Assistance in 2026?

$820 per household for disasters declared on or after October 1, 2026, and $790 for disasters declared from October 1, 2025 to September 30, 2026.

Do I have to pay back Serious Needs Assistance?

Generally no, unless the same need was also paid by insurance or another program.

How do I get the FEMA $820 payment?

Apply for FEMA assistance in a declared disaster area and show you have urgent disaster-caused needs such as food, water, or medicine. FEMA decides eligibility.
