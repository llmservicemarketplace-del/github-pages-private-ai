National Disaster Assistance Resources

  • DisasterAssistance.gov (Official FEMA Individual Assistance Application) — Apply online for federal disaster grants and check declaration status.
  • 211.org (Community Health & Human Services Directory) — Dial 211 or search online for local emergency shelters, food banks, and disaster relief.
  • Ready.gov (Official Emergency Preparedness & Recovery) — Comprehensive public safety instructions for severe weather hazards.
