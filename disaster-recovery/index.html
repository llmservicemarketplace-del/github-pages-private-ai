Disaster Recovery Resources

Following hurricanes, tornadoes, hail, fire, or flooding, clear safety information can help families protect life, prevent more damage, apply for assistance, and organize the next step. These guides are free to read and do not require a purchase, donation, or software account.

Emergency: If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 or the appropriate local emergency authority. Treat downed power lines, gas odors, fire, unsafe structures, floodwater, and electrical hazards as emergencies. NFCcop does not dispatch emergency responders.

National Disaster Assistance Resources

  • DisasterAssistance.gov (Official FEMA Individual Assistance Application) — Apply online for federal disaster grants and check declaration status.
  • 211.org (Community Health & Human Services Directory) — Dial 211 or search online for local emergency shelters, food banks, and disaster relief.
  • Ready.gov (Official Emergency Preparedness & Recovery) — Comprehensive public safety instructions for severe weather hazards.
