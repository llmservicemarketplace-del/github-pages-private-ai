Two programs, one principle

PAi Legal builds the case. PAi Claims builds the claim. Both free to download.

PAi Legal

For firms and for the self-represented. Index, organize, build the case.

  • Drop in complaints, motions, depositions, exhibits — OCR makes every word searchable
  • Separates what is alleged from what your documents actually prove
  • Names the missing element of each claim before a judge does
  • One button switches the whole interface between attorney and plain language, mid-case
  • Every matter in its own folder or drive — nothing pools, nothing crosses
  • Optional API help for drafting court documents — for a real filing, a frontier model handles the nuance a local model can't; it warns before anything is shared, and what leaves is your choice

Free download · first case free

The engine and local model keep your case private and do the analysis. But a document a court will actually read turns on nuance — tone, emphasis, how a fact is characterized, what goes unsaid. That is where a frontier model earns its place, so the option is there when the filing is real. Nothing is shared without a warning and your say-so.

PAi Claims

Build the claim from evidence — before the carrier defines it.

As a policyholder, you can have your own itemized estimate in the adjuster's hands before he even pulls into the driveway — so he has to account for every screw, nail, texture match and cleanup line, instead of writing the number and leaving you to argue it.

  • Policy, photos, measurements, estimates and letters in one connected record
  • Builds an independent, itemized repair estimate from real regional pricing
  • Keeps damage, quantity, method, material and price as separately supportable facts
  • Compares the carrier's estimate against yours for scope, pricing and omissions
  • Turns the finished claim into a demand letter — and a court filing if you have to
  • Asks whether you want to use a local AI or your own API key — a tablet or an old computer is enough
  • Learns across claims — pricing, carrier patterns and outcomes carry forward as data, not as lineage, so it gets sharper the more it sees without an old claim becoming proof in a new one

Commonly missed or under-scoped items on an insurance estimate

These are the line items adjusters most often leave off a first estimate. PAi Claims builds each one back into the scope, from evidence.

1 · Water heater replacement & plumbing

Plumbing permit and inspection fees, expansion tank, code upgrades (relief valve, pipe routing, seismic strapping, elevated platform or stand, drip pan with PVC drain line), valves and connectors, electrical or gas disconnect and reconnect, haul-away and disposal.

2 · Water mitigation & drying

Emergency service call and after-hours fee, water extraction, dehumidifiers, air movers and fans, HEPA air scrubber, equipment setup, monitoring and takedown, antimicrobial or biocide application.

3 · Demolition & containment

Containment barriers, floor protection, the difference between a 4-foot and a 2-foot flood cut, insulation removal and replacement, dumpster or roll-off rental and disposal, content manipulation.

4 · Drywall & finishing

Drywall backer and blocking, a proper 3-coat mud and tape finish, texture matching, corner beads.

5 · Painting — the continuous wall rule

PVA primer and sealer, full-wall painting corner-to-corner rather than spot patching, masking and prep.

6 · Baseboards & millwork

Baseboard tear-out and replacement, shoe molding and quarter round, caulking and spackling, priming and painting baseboards, door jambs and casings.

7 · Electrical

Outlet and switch replacement, testing and inspection.

8 · General contractor overhead & profit (O&P)

10% overhead plus 10% profit, which applies when three or more trades are involved. Adjusters often omit General Contractor O&P on the initial estimate even when multiple trades are clearly required.

Why the AI here is an asset, not a risk

Take away the one thing that causes fabricated citations and confident wrong answers — letting the model decide what is true.

Traced

Every fact points to its source

Facts come out of your own documents, built sentence by sentence. If the software shows you a case, that case exists and you can open it.

Private

Nothing leaves your computer

Both programs run on your Windows machine. Case files, OCR text and work product stay in the folder or drive you pick. A private model is set up for you and runs locally.

Replayable

The same file gives the same result

The analysis is computed from the record, not sampled from a model, and carries a replay signature — so what you show a judge is what the software produced.

Nobody should lose their home because an insurer counted on them not fighting back.

When a storm takes the roof off a house, the family under it is suddenly negotiating with a company that does this every day — backed by adjusters and lawyers — while they do it for the first time in the worst week of their life. NFCcop exists to close that gap: the same documentation and organization a professional would bring, made free and usable by the person who actually needs it.

That is why PAi exists and why the first case is free. The software is carried by the nonprofit and funded by donations, so it can reach the people who need it most and can least afford help — disaster survivors, and households facing a denial alone. Every donation is tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law.
