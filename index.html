How it works

This is the setup path for the specialist chat assistants. PAi Claims and PAi Legal are separate standalone applications and install directly. Internet is needed once, during setup. After that, never.

  1. Buy the assistant you want and receive an activation code.
  2. Download the PAi setup program — one file, about 40 MB.
  3. Enter your code. PAi checks your computer’s memory, disk space and graphics, then recommends the AI that suits it.
  4. Choose your AI engine — DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama or Gemma.
  5. The AI downloads. Several gigabytes, once, because it runs on your machine rather than a server. It resumes if interrupted.
  6. Done. PAi installs it, tests that it starts, and creates your desktop shortcut. Disconnect the internet if you like.

Frequently asked questions

Does PAi send my data to the cloud?

No. After the one-time model download during setup, everything runs locally on your computer. Your prompts, files and conversation history never leave your machine.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No. There is no account, no monthly fee and no telemetry. Once installed, the software works with the internet switched off.

What are the system requirements?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 8 GB RAM minimum (16 GB recommended), and about 10 GB free disk space. No Python, Docker or developer tools required.

Is the AI model included?

You choose and download an open model (DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama or Gemma) directly from its publisher under that publisher’s licence. PAi handles the guided install and configuration.

Can I use it completely offline?

Yes. Once the model is installed, PAi works with the internet disconnected.
