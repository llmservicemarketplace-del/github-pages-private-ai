Denied coverage or a low repair offer

Denied or Underpaid Property Insurance Claims

The denial arrived. The repair costs still remain. Start with the insurer’s written reasons. Compare them with your policy, photos, measurements, and contractor estimates.

  • Denied coverage? Address the reason the insurer gives.
  • Low estimate? Identify missing repairs, quantities, prices, and depreciation questions.
  • Scattered records? Gather the policy, correspondence, photos, and receipts in one file.

Court papers, evidence, and deadlines

Self-Represented Eviction & Lawsuit Defense

Court papers can turn an ordinary day into a deadline. If you face eviction or a lawsuit without a lawyer, identify the court, hearing date, and response requirements first.

Keep the notice, complaint, lease or contract, payment records, and correspondence together. An organized file also helps a legal aid provider understand your situation.

A clearer view of a complicated history

Offline Medical Record & FDA Label Tracking

Your health history should not be scattered across portals and paperwork. Organize visit summaries, lab results, medications, and daily readings. Find the source behind an entry and prepare questions for your next appointment.

Keep records together with PAi Health

PAi Health provides a local Windows workspace for personal health information and source-linked FDA medication-label context. Local records do not require cloud storage; retrieving or refreshing online references may require an internet connection.

A label match does not prove a medication caused a symptom. Ask a pharmacist or prescriber before changing treatment.

The NFCcop Software Access Program

Cost Should Not Stop You From Asking for Help

If you are a low-income disaster survivor, a policyholder facing a denied claim, or someone preparing court records without a lawyer, request 100% free PAi Claims or PAi Legal software access.

No purchase, donation, or paid membership is required to request help. Approved free access carries no PAi software fee for the access provided.

Our public guides remain free to read. For PAi Health assistance, email us to discuss available options.

See the free-access program details →

How to request free access

  1. Email support@yprivateai.com.
  2. Use the subject “Request for free software access.”
  3. Include your preferred name, the tool you need, and a short explanation that cost is a barrier.
  4. Our team will reply with instructions or questions needed to arrange assistance.

Do not send identification documents, bank statements, policy numbers, medical records, or your full case file in the first email.

Sending an email does not automatically activate software. Optional external AI providers may charge separately.

Email a free-access request

Deadlines come first. Do not wait for an installation or support reply if a hearing, filing, or insurance deadline is approaching. Seek qualified help promptly.
