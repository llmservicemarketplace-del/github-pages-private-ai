Verify before paying

Real-Estate Listing and Wire-Fraud Scam Prevention

Verify listings, owners, agents, landlords, title and escrow companies, and payment instructions before sending personal information or money.

Time-sensitive: If money may have been sent to a fraudster, contact the sending bank or payment provider immediately and ask about a recall or fraud hold. Then preserve all messages and report the incident through appropriate law-enforcement and fraud-reporting channels.

The short answer

A professional-looking listing, email chain, document, or caller ID can be copied or impersonated. Verify the property, owner, representative, company, and payment instructions through sources you locate independently. A last-minute change to wiring instructions is a reason to stop and verify—not a reason to hurry.

Independent verification routine

Verify the property

Compare the listing across platforms and check the owner, parcel, tax, and recording information through official local records.

Verify each person

Locate the brokerage, landlord, lender, title company, or closing company through an independent directory or regulator.

Verify communications

Inspect domain spelling, reply addresses, telephone numbers, pressure tactics, and requests to move to an unusual messaging channel.

Verify payment

Call a known number and confirm the recipient, bank, account, amount, and purpose before sending funds. Repeat the check after any change.

Preserve evidence

Save the original listing URL, screenshots, headers, texts, telephone numbers, documents, receipts, account details, and timeline.

Before sending money or identification

  • Property and owner verified in official records
  • Representative and company verified independently
  • Email domain and telephone number checked
  • Written agreement reviewed and questions answered
  • Payment recipient and instructions confirmed by a second channel
  • Sensitive documents transmitted only through a trusted method

Print or save this list. Add dates and source information so you can tell what was known at each decision point.

Watch for scams and unsafe shortcuts

  • A below-market listing paired with extreme urgency
  • Refusal to meet, show the property, or permit independent verification
  • A last-minute change in title, escrow, bank, or wire instructions
  • Payment demanded through gift cards, cryptocurrency, cash courier, or another unusual irreversible method

Safety rule: Verify the person, organization, property, ownership, and payment instructions through an independently obtained source before sending documents or money.
