Rebuild or Relocate: A Decision and Documentation Guide

Compare rebuilding and relocation using verified costs, insurance information, mortgage obligations, timelines, hazards, and household needs.

The short answer

The right decision cannot be reduced to one estimate. Compare both pathways using the same categories: available funds, policy and mortgage constraints, repair or purchase costs, time, temporary housing, future hazards, insurance availability, household needs, and uncertainty. Mark missing values as unknown rather than treating them as zero.

Create two evidence-based scenarios

Separate verified facts from assumptions

Label each amount and date as documented, estimated, disputed, pending, or unknown.

Build the rebuild scenario

Include demolition, design, permits, code upgrades, construction, delays, temporary housing, financing, deductibles, and future insurance.

Build the relocate scenario

Include sale or disposition of the damaged property, payoff questions, moving, deposits, closing, repairs, travel, taxes, and replacement insurance.

Compare nonfinancial needs

Record safety, health, accessibility, schools, work, caregiving, community, transportation, and emotional considerations.

Set review points

Update the comparison when a coverage position, estimate, permit decision, financing term, or housing deadline changes.

Decision file

  • Policy documents and written insurer positions
  • Mortgage balance, payment status, and servicer correspondence
  • Repair, demolition, design, permit, and code estimates
  • Temporary-housing duration and documented expenses
  • Replacement-property, moving, closing, repair, and insurance estimates
  • Hazard, access, family, work, school, health, and support-network considerations
  • Unknown or disputed items clearly preserved as unknown or disputed

Print or save this list. Add dates and source information so you can tell what was known at each decision point.

Watch for scams and unsafe shortcuts

  • A contractor or buyer pressuring you to decide before the scope and authority are verified
  • Someone treating an unpaid, disputed, or unknown insurance amount as guaranteed cash
  • A quick-sale or deed-transfer offer that avoids independent title and mortgage review
  • A promise that rebuilding or relocating will automatically solve insurance or financial problems

Safety rule: Verify the person, organization, property, ownership, and payment instructions through an independently obtained source before sending documents or money.
