Emergency-to-permanent housing

Finding Replacement Housing After a Disaster

Move from emergency lodging to stable replacement housing while tracking expenses, evaluating needs, checking risks, and avoiding rental scams.

The short answer

Treat emergency lodging, transitional housing, and permanent replacement housing as separate decisions. First protect immediate safety and continuity. Then document temporary expenses and requirements. Only after that should you commit to a long-term location, lease, or purchase based on verified affordability, hazards, insurance, access, and household needs.

Three housing horizons

Emergency

Prioritize safe shelter, medication, accessible facilities, pets, transportation, communications, and registration with legitimate assistance programs.

Transitional

Seek a workable lease or extended stay while tracking receipts, deposits, mileage, meals, laundry, storage, and insurance communications.

Permanent

Compare neighborhoods and properties using full costs, hazard information, insurance availability, condition, access, and long-term household needs.

Document

Keep written reasons for housing choices, declined options, extensions, and unavoidable extra costs.

Housing-needs worksheet

  • Number of occupants, beds, accessibility, and medical requirements
  • Pet, school, work, transportation, and support-network needs
  • Maximum total monthly cost—not rent or mortgage alone
  • Deposit, application, utility, storage, travel, and moving costs
  • Lease length and realistic recovery timeline
  • Receipts, communications, and reasons each option was accepted or rejected

Print or save this list. Add dates and source information so you can tell what was known at each decision point.

Watch for scams and unsafe shortcuts

  • A rental copied from a real listing but offered by an impostor
  • A landlord who cannot show the unit or verify authority to rent it
  • Requests to pay by gift card, cryptocurrency, or an irreversible transfer
  • Pressure to skip the written lease or send identification through an insecure channel

Safety rule: Verify the person, organization, property, ownership, and payment instructions through an independently obtained source before sending documents or money.
