PAi Photography

Private assistant for cameras, lenses, shooting technique, lighting and editing workflow.

Buy PAi Photography I already have a code

What you can ask it

  • Help me get the exposure I want in this situation
  • Explain what this setting actually does
  • Help me choose between these lenses for what I shoot
  • Why are my images soft, noisy or badly lit?
  • Help me plan a lighting setup with what I own
  • Walk me through a sensible editing workflow
  • How should I test a used camera or lens before buying?
  • Help me price and describe gear I am selling
  • What is the best way to organise and back up my photos?

It runs on your computer, not a server

PAi Photography loads an AI model onto your own Windows PC during setup and runs it there. After installation it works with the network disconnected. Nothing you type is uploaded, logged or used for training, and there is no account and no subscription.

You choose the engine during setup from Qwen, Gemma, Llama, DeepSeek, and PAi checks your memory, disk space and graphics first so it only offers what your machine can actually run. The recommended tier for this assistant is Standard.

How to get it running

  • Buy PAi Photography and receive an activation code.
  • Install PAi from the Microsoft Store.
  • Open it, enter your code, and let it check your hardware.
  • Pick an engine. PAi downloads and configures it for you.

What it will not do

Gear specifications, prices and firmware change constantly and this assistant runs offline — verify anything model-specific against the manufacturer. Photographing people and property carries privacy and permission obligations that vary by country.
