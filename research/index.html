Insurance Claim Denied After a Storm? Start With These Answers

Ask for the insurer's reasons in writing, match each disputed point to your policy and records, and prepare a specific response. The CFPB recommends requesting written reasons and asking about the appeals process. Then identify which question you need to resolve:

  • Was coverage denied? Identify the cited exclusion or condition and the facts the carrier relied on. Put the policy language beside your loss evidence. Read the denial letter step by step.
  • Does the estimate miss work? Compare rooms, materials, measurements and repair tasks, rather than only the final total. Mark each difference and its supporting source. Compare insurance estimates.
  • Is the payment lower than the estimate? Separate the deductible, depreciation, prior payments and any stated limits before deciding what is disputed. Understand replacement cost and actual cash value.
  • Are calls or submissions unanswered? Make a dated log, preserve delivery confirmations and ask a specific written question. Prepare a regulator complaint if the issue remains unresolved.
