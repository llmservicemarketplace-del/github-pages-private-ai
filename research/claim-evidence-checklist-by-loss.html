What evidence do I need for my type of home insurance claim?

Leer en español

Every claim needs the basics: the policy, photos, a timeline and receipts. Beyond that, each type of loss has its own evidence that decides it. Use the list for your loss.

The short answer. Photograph before you clean up or repair, keep damaged items until the insurer has seen them, and record dates. Then gather the specific evidence for your type of loss below.

Every claim

  • Certified copy of the policy, declarations and endorsements. See requesting your policy.
  • Date and time of loss, and when you reported it.
  • Wide and close-up photos and video of all damage, before cleanup.
  • Receipts for emergency repairs and mitigation.
  • Contractor estimates, itemized.
  • A claim log of every contact.
  • A contents inventory.

Wind

  • Weather reports for the date: National Weather Service storm reports and wind speeds near your address.
  • Photos of lifted, creased or missing shingles, and debris in the yard.
  • Fallen trees and limbs, with where they landed. See tree fell on house.
  • Interior water stains that appeared after the storm.

Hail

  • Hail reports and hail size for the date and location.
  • Photos of hits on soft metals (vents, gutters, AC fins) and on the roof, with chalk circles and a scale.
  • A roofer's test-square count per slope.
  • Notes on functional vs. cosmetic damage. See cosmetic damage.

Water (plumbing)

  • The failed part, kept and photographed.
  • Plumber's written cause of loss.
  • Moisture readings and drying logs.
  • Water bills around the loss date. See sudden vs. gradual.

Fire and smoke

  • Fire department report number and incident report.
  • Photos of smoke and soot in rooms the fire didn't reach.
  • Cleaning or testing reports for soot and odor.
  • ALE receipts if you can't live in the home. See house fire recovery.

Flood

  • Flood policy (often NFIP) separate from homeowners. See flood insurance basics.
  • Photos of the water line on walls, inside and out, with a tape measure.
  • Proof of loss deadline for NFIP claims. See NFIP proof of loss.
  • If there was also wind damage, separate evidence of each. See wind vs. flood.

Theft and vandalism

  • Police report number.
  • Proof of ownership: receipts, photos, serial numbers, bank or card statements.
  • Photos of the point of entry and damage.

Quick answers

What evidence do I need for a home insurance claim?

The policy, dated photos and video taken before cleanup, receipts, itemized estimates and a log of every contact, plus the specific evidence for your type of loss.

Should I throw out damaged items?

Not until the insurer has seen them or agreed in writing. Photograph them and keep samples if you must dispose of them for health reasons.

How do I prove hail or wind hit my house on a certain date?

Use National Weather Service storm reports and hail size data for your location and date, together with photos and a roofer's inspection.
