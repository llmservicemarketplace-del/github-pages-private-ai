When does an insurer’s handling of a claim cross into bad faith?

An insurer owes more than the policy limit: it owes reasonable handling. Failing to investigate, ignoring its own engineer, sitting on a claim past statutory deadlines, or denying without a stated basis can expose a carrier beyond what the policy would have paid.

Standards and remedies differ sharply by state. Some provide a statutory cause of action with attorney fees, some require a showing closer to intent, and some require notice and a cure period before suit.

Document the timeline. Dates of contact, what was requested, what was provided and what the carrier did next are the whole case.

Rules differ by state and by policy wording. Use the searches below to read what courts in your own state have said, then check that a case is still good law before relying on it.
