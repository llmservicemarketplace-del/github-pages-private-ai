My insurer says the hail damage is only cosmetic. What does that mean?

Some policies exclude hail damage that changes only how a surface looks, such as dents in a metal roof, while still covering damage that affects how it works. If your policy has this endorsement, the dispute becomes whether the damage is "cosmetic" or "functional."

The short answer. Find the exact wording of the endorsement and what it covers (often only metal roofing or certain surfaces). Then document any functional damage: anything that lets water in, shortens the material's life, or breaks seams, fasteners or coatings.

Cosmetic vs. functional

Usually called cosmeticOften argued as functional
Small dents in metal panels with no breachCracked or split panels, punctures, broken seams
Paint scuffs on gutters or sidingDamaged or loosened fasteners and sealant
Marks that don't affect drainageDents that hold water or damage protective coatings
Damage to flashing, vents or the panels' locking edges

The precise definition is in your endorsement. Courts read that wording closely, and results vary by the facts and the state.

What to do

  1. Get a certified copy of the endorsement. See requesting your policy.
  2. Check which surfaces it applies to. Some endorsements cover only metal roofing; your shingles, siding or windows may be fully covered.
  3. Have a roofer or engineer inspect for functional damage and write it up with close-up photos and measurements.
  4. Ask for any engineer report the insurer relied on. See engineer reports.
  5. If you disagree with the conclusion, respond in writing with your evidence.

Before you buy or renew

If you have a metal roof in a hail area, ask your agent whether your policy has a cosmetic exclusion and whether removing it is available.

Quick answers

What does cosmetic damage mean in a homeowners policy?

It generally means damage that changes appearance without affecting how the material works, such as dents in a metal roof that don't let water in. The exact definition is in your endorsement.

Does a cosmetic exclusion apply to my whole house?

Not necessarily. Many endorsements apply only to certain surfaces, such as metal roofing. Read the endorsement.

How do I prove hail damage is functional?

Have a roofer or engineer document breaches, cracked or split panels, damaged seams, fasteners or coatings, with close-up photos and measurements.
