How do I file a home insurance claim, and how fast do I have to report it?

Most homeowners policies require prompt notice of a loss. The policy rarely gives a number of days, but waiting can give the insurer a reason to dispute or deny the claim, especially if the delay made the damage worse or harder to inspect.

Some states also set outside deadlines. In Florida, for example, notice of a new or reopened property claim must be given within one year of the date of loss, and a supplemental claim within 18 months, or the claim is barred.

The short answer. Report the loss as soon as you can, in writing as well as by phone. Write down the claim number, the adjuster's name, and every date. Protect the property from more damage, photograph everything before cleanup, and keep every receipt. Rules differ by state and by policy wording.

What this turns on

  • What your policy's "duties after loss" section requires.
  • Whether your state sets its own notice deadline.
  • Whether the damage got worse while you waited to report it.

Your first-week checklist

  1. Call your insurer or agent and open a claim. Get the claim number.
  2. Follow up in writing (email or portal) with the date of loss, cause, and a short description.
  3. Photograph and video everything before cleanup. See how to document damage.
  4. Make temporary repairs to stop further damage, and keep the receipts. See your duty to mitigate.
  5. Keep damaged items until the adjuster has seen them, unless they are a health hazard. Photograph them first.
  6. Request a full copy of your policy, including all endorsements.
  7. Start a claim log of every call, email, and visit.

What your file needs

  • The date of loss and cause (storm name, leak source, fire).
  • Photos and video before and after cleanup.
  • Receipts for emergency repairs and extra living costs.
  • Your complete policy and declarations page.

Quick answers

How long do I have to file a home insurance claim?

Most policies require prompt notice. Some states add a hard deadline: in Florida, new or reopened claims must be reported within one year of the date of loss and supplemental claims within 18 months.

Should I report a claim in writing?

Yes. Call to open the claim, then confirm in writing so you have a dated record.
