Where it goes

We develop tools and resources to help disaster survivors organize property-loss records, understand recovery steps, and prepare supporting documentation.

  • Recovery resources for disaster survivors Our research and resource pages are free to read. Software downloads are free; claim and case creation charges are explained on our pricing page.
  • Software that shows its work Our tools help organize source material and distinguish supported information from estimates and unresolved questions. Review generated work against your original records before relying on it.
  • Supporting recovery resources Voluntary donations support development and outreach. Donations are separate from required software purchases, and you do not need to donate to use the software.
