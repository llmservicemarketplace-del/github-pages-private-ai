Where it goes

We build and give away tools that help people document a property loss accurately, so an insurance settlement rests on evidence rather than on whoever has more lawyers.

  • Free claim documentation for disaster survivors Our software is offered at no cost through disaster relief and voluntary agency channels, to households working a claim without a public adjuster or attorney.
  • Software that shows its work Every figure our tools produce is traced to the page it came from, and marked with how far it can be trusted. Nothing is asserted that cannot be shown.
  • Supporting recovery resources Development, hosting, and support are funded by gifts like yours rather than by charging the people who need the tools most.
