How to Get an Itemized Hospital Bill

Last reviewed November 8, 2026. Free public guidance from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. This is general information, not legal or medical advice. Rules can change, so confirm with the official sources below.

After a hospital stay, the first bill you get is often a summary bill: a few totals such as “pharmacy” or “room and board.” You can’t check a bill like that. An itemized bill lists every service, test, medicine, and supply with its own charge and code. Hospitals routinely provide one when asked, and many states require them to.

How to ask for it

  1. Call the number on your bill and say: “Please send me a fully itemized bill with CPT or HCPCS codes and revenue codes for every charge.”
  2. Ask them to hold the account while you review it, so it isn’t sent to collections.
  3. Follow up in writing. Send a short letter or portal message with your account number and the dates of the stay. Keep a copy.
  4. Ask for your medical records too. Comparing the bill with your records shows whether you got what you were billed for. See how to request your medical records.

What to look for

  • Duplicates: the same test or medicine billed twice on the same day.
  • Days that don’t match: room charges for the day you went home, or for more days than you stayed.
  • Things you didn’t get: a medicine you refused, or a test that was canceled.
  • Very high supply charges: basic items billed at high prices. Ask what they are.
  • Upcoding: a visit billed at a more complex level than what happened.

If you find a problem, see how to dispute a medical billing error.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay to get an itemized bill?

Hospitals usually provide an itemized bill for free when you ask. If you're charged, ask why and check your state's rules.

What is the difference between a summary bill and an itemized bill?

A summary bill shows totals by category. An itemized bill lists every service, test, medicine, and supply separately, with codes, so you can check each one.

Can I ask for an itemized bill after I've paid?

Yes. If you find an error, you can ask for a correction and a refund.
