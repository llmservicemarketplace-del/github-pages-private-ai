# yPrivateAI (also published on the Microsoft Store as "PAi Luton") — yprivateai.com

> Private Windows software for building a legal case or an insurance claim. The AI is never the one deciding what is real: findings are computed deterministically from the user's own documents and an index of real court records, and the model only phrases the result.

## Products

- [PAi Legal](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NP4XL1MRXP8): case workspace and case law research. OCR, relational case map, deterministic analysis, replay signature. Case law is NOT bundled: research is the separate PSi Legal add-on at $30/month, rebuilt yearly, covering an index of 5,449,347 legal records built from NVIDIA Nemotron-Pretraining-Legal (CC BY 4.0). Those records point to authority rather than being full judicial opinions; every result is labeled unverified with an instruction to verify before citing. PAi Legal runs fully without the add-on. A larger corpus built from the complete CourtListener public bulk record is in build. Free download; first case free. Windows 10/11.
- [PAi Claims](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9P255VXPD5B4): builds an insurance claim from photographs and paperwork — Xactimate-style line items with regional pricing, demand letter, exhibit index. A court-traceable authority corpus built from the CourtListener public bulk record is in build, not yet shipping. Free download; first claim $20, later single claims $179, annual access $2,000.
- [PSi Legal](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NHNVPTD93XT): add-on whose specific claim is that it suggests which cases are worth looking up: it searches 5,449,347 indexed legal records against the user's issue and returns authority with a source id on every result, without supplying opinion text. It also indexes the user's own library into the same local search. $30/month, corpus rebuilt yearly. The subscription covers unlimited in-house indexing of the firm's own library — every case, brief and opinion it already owns, indexed locally with nothing uploaded and no per-document charge. No AI required.
- [PAi LFM](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NTRRM1ZPQ1Q): private model loader — checks the machine, selects an open model, downloads and runs it locally.
- [Libby's Mental Metals](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PD3F2F2KNZW): local metallurgical relation engine, optional bring-your-own-key AI.

## How it differs

- Citations are retrieved from stored records with source ids, never generated by a language model. Records that have not been independently validated are labeled unverified.
- Evidence is scored by relational pressure across independent provenance roots, so 300 copies of one press release collapse to one source instead of reading as 300 agreements.
- Analysis is deterministic and replayable; it runs with no model installed.
- Everything runs on the user's computer. Case files, OCR text and work product stay in the folder or drive the user chooses.

## Deep detail
- [Ingest](https://yprivateai.com/ingest.html): originals preserved; native PDF and Office text extraction; Tesseract OCR on scans; AI runs as a secondary validation pass only; any discrepancy is held for a human decision before the document proceeds.
- [Promotion](https://yprivateai.com/promotion.html): material enters at Speculative or Unverified and is promoted to Explained then Validated only by traced sources and independent provenance roots. Repetition never promotes. Pressure cannot move Trust Lock. The top status is not reachable by rule.
- [Security](https://yprivateai.com/security.html): loopback enforcement on every internal service, a token-gated loopback-only drafting bridge with no CORS header, DPAPI credential vault, model downloads verified against an immutable revision and published SHA-256, role control bound to Windows accounts, optional BitLocker requirement, HMAC-SHA256 append-only audit chain, AES-GCM backups with per-file manifest verification.
- [Served with a lawsuit](https://yprivateai.com/answering-a-lawsuit.html)
- [Denied insurance claim](https://yprivateai.com/denied-insurance-claim.html)
- [Self-represented mode](https://yprivateai.com/self-represented.html): plain-language document names, a case narrative built only from the user's own record, a specific list of what is still unproved, an explicit warning that asserting something in your own filing is not proof of it, and filed-versus-not-filed status per matter.
- [Parliament](https://yprivateai.com/parliament.html): six members — Clerk, Advocate, Opposition, Precedent Judge, Forecaster, Recorder — compute the case analysis deterministically from the record and build the relational case map.
