Our mission and how it's funded

Our goal: help 100,000 disaster survivors prepare clear claim documentation, free. This is our goal, not a count of people already served.

What we do

After a storm, the insurance company shows up with estimating software and a number. The homeowner shows up with phone photos. We fix that.

PAi Claims prices damage line by line, with quantities, unit prices, overhead and profit, depreciation and code upgrades. It works like the estimating tools insurers use, but it's built for the homeowner and the car owner. Every number stays tied to the photo or document behind it. It runs on your own computer.

Where the money goes

  • Software fees from PAi Claims and PAi Legal go to The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. They fund development and free copies for survivors who can't pay.
  • Donations fund outreach: getting the software to disaster-hit communities through relief and legal-aid partners.
  • Free for everyone: our guides, research and resource directory. No purchase or donation required.

Software purchases and donations are separate. No purchase or donation is required to read our guides or request assistance. Terms are on our pricing page.
