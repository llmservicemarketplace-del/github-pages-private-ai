What is available without paying

Our online recovery guides, research explainers, and resource directory are available without a purchase or donation. If you cannot afford PAi software, contact us to request free access.

Software fees and voluntary donations

Software downloads are free. Standard PAi Claims access charges for creating claims; the first PAi Legal case is free and additional case creation is paid. See the pricing page for published terms. Required software payments are purchases, separate from voluntary donations.

Donations support the disaster-recovery work described on our programs page, including resources, software development, and outreach. Donations are handled through the secure Zeffy link on our donation page. No donation is required to read our resources or request assistance.

The organization and the software

yPrivateAI presents a program of The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc., based in Ray City, Georgia. The software is published under PAi Luton. This website identifies the nonprofit as a 501(c)(3) organization, EIN 33-3049015.

This update does not claim that a particular percentage of future receipts will be spent on any category, or that development costs have been reimbursed. Questions about software proceeds, costs, and available organizational records can be directed to support@yprivateai.com.

Questions about a donation

Follow Zeffy’s confirmation and receipt instructions after donating. For questions about the mission or intended use of gifts, contact the organization. Consult a tax professional about your own deduction eligibility.
