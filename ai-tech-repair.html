PAi Tech Repair

Private assistant for diagnosing and repairing computers, phones, tablets, networks and consumer electronics.

What you can ask it

  • Help me diagnose a computer that will not power on
  • My phone will not charge — how do I narrow it down?
  • Help me work out whether this is worth repairing
  • Walk me through recovering data from a failing drive
  • My wifi keeps dropping — how do I find the cause?
  • Is this a software problem or a hardware problem?
  • Help me test this device safely before I buy or sell it
  • What tools and parts do I need for this repair?
  • Explain what this error or beep code means

It runs on your computer, not a server

PAi Tech Repair loads an AI model onto your own Windows PC during setup and runs it there. After installation it works with the network disconnected. Nothing you type is uploaded, logged or used for training, and there is no account and no subscription.

You choose the engine during setup from Qwen, Gemma, Llama, DeepSeek, and PAi checks your memory, disk space and graphics first so it only offers what your machine can actually run. The recommended tier for this assistant is Standard.

How to get it running

  • Buy PAi Tech Repair and receive an activation code.
  • Install PAi from the Microsoft Store.
  • Open it, enter your code, and let it check your hardware.
  • Pick an engine. PAi downloads and configures it for you.

What it will not do

Electronics repair involves shock, burn, battery-fire and data-loss risks. Mains equipment and capacitors can hold lethal charge after unplugging. Back up data before any repair attempt, and stop if a lithium battery is swollen, punctured or hot. Opening a device may void its warranty.
