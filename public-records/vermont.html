Vermont Court and Public Records Search

Links checked November 10, 2026. A free directory from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We link only to official government sources and free nonprofit tools. This is general information, not legal advice.

Use this page to find official, free places to search Vermont court cases, business filings, property records, and more. Many records are kept by county offices, so you'll often need to know the county where something happened.

Use public records responsibly. Information from these sites can be incomplete or out of date, and names can match the wrong person. Federal law (the Fair Credit Reporting Act) limits using records to screen someone for a job, rental, credit, or insurance unless you follow its rules. Never use records to harass, threaten, or stalk anyone.

Property, deeds, and liens

Property records in Vermont are kept county by county. Search the county's official website for these offices:

Frequently asked questions

Can I search Vermont court records for free?

Yes, in many cases. Vermont Judiciary Public Portal is a statewide option. You can also search the clerk of court in the county where the case was filed.

How do I look up a business in Vermont?

Use the Vermont business search search to find a company's status, registered agent, and filings.

How do I request public records in Vermont?

Under the Vermont Public Records Act, send a written request to the agency that keeps the record. Agencies may charge copy fees and may withhold some records by law.

Where are federal court cases from Vermont found?

In PACER, the federal courts' records system, which charges a small per-page fee. Many federal dockets are also free on CourtListener.
