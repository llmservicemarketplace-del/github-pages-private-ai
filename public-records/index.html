Free Court and Public Records Research by State

Links checked November 10, 2026. A free directory from The New Faith Church and Co-op, Inc. (NFCcop), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Official government sources and free nonprofit tools only.

Look up court cases, businesses, property records, inmates, and more, using official and free sources. Choose your state for its court, Secretary of State, property, and prison record links, or use the national tools below.

Use public records responsibly. Information from these sites can be incomplete or out of date, and names can match the wrong person. Federal law (the Fair Credit Reporting Act) limits using records to screen someone for a job, rental, credit, or insurance unless you follow its rules. Never use records to harass, threaten, or stalk anyone.

Tips for better searches

  • Start with the county. Most court, property, and deed records are kept by county offices.
  • Try name variations: middle initials, maiden names, and business names with and without "LLC" or "Inc."
  • Confirm with the clerk. Online indexes can lag or leave things out. Ask the clerk for certified copies if you need them for court.
  • Ask for records in writing under your state's public records law when something isn't online.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I search court records for free?

Many states have free statewide case searches, and most county clerks have free online search. Federal cases are in PACER (small fee) and many are free on CourtListener. Pick your state above.

How do I look up who owns a business?

Search the Secretary of State (or corporations division) business search in the state where the company is registered. It shows the registered agent and, in many states, officers or managers.

How do I find someone in jail or prison?

Use the state Department of Corrections search for state prisons, the county sheriff's site for jails, the Federal Bureau of Prisons locator for federal custody, or VINELink.

Are these records always accurate?

No. Records can be incomplete, delayed, or match the wrong person with the same name. Confirm important information with the court clerk or agency.
