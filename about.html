Our story & mission

Built out of need.
Built to help others.

A legal dispute. A hurricane. A tree through a home. PAi Claims and PAi Legal grew from the need to make sense of the records and take the next step.

Why we do this

Recovery takes more than repairs.

People also need their photographs, documents, estimates, and correspondence in order. NFCcop helps make that work more manageable through practical software and free educational resources.

The experience behind the tools

Keith Darrell Luton

Creator of PAi Claims and PAi Legal
Ray City, Georgia

I was already dealing with an intellectual-property dispute with IBM when Hurricane Helene hit. A tree fell on my house. Suddenly, I was trying to deal with damage to my home while I was still working through a legal dispute.

From my perspective, the insurance company's response amounted to a denial of the help I needed to recover. I had to work through the damage, the claim, and the records supporting it.

Those experiences are why I built PAi Claims and PAi Legal. I needed a way to organize the material, follow the connections between documents, and prepare the next step. I built these products out of need.

I wanted the tools I needed to be useful to other people facing the same kind of work.

Through The New Faith Church and Co-op, that work supports a broader mission: helping people document what happened, understand the questions they need to ask, and move forward after a disaster.

Keith Darrell Luton

This is Keith's personal account of the experiences that led to the products. It does not describe a court finding or claim an affiliation with IBM.

Who we serve

People facing a loss and the paperwork that follows.

Our disaster-recovery work is for people affected by hurricanes, floods, fires, and other disasters, including those organizing an insurance claim or handling related legal records themselves.

We aim to make useful technology and information easier to access when money, time, and attention are already stretched.

PAi Claims offers a choice of local AI or an external AI provider during setup. The privacy policy explains local storage and optional external services.
