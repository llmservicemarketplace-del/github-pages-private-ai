Help people document, recover, and rebuild.

After a storm, a fire, or a flood, most families face an insurance estimate they have no way to check. We develop tools to help people organize the evidence and compare estimates, alongside free online research and recovery resources.

Donate money, equipment, or property

The New Faith Church and Co-op Inc. welcomes gifts that support our programs. You can give money through Zeffy or contact us about donating vehicles, computers, tools, household goods, land, or other property.

Donate money Offer an item or property

Vehicles

Cars, trucks, vans, motorcycles, boats, trailers, RVs, and farm equipment. Contact us about running or non-running vehicles and tell us whether you have the title.

Computers and electronics

Desktops, laptops, servers, monitors, networking equipment, phones, and tablets in working or repairable condition. Contact us to discuss equipment condition and storage-device handling.

Tools and equipment

Hand and power tools, shop equipment, generators, compressors, ladders, and usable building materials.

Land and real property

Vacant land, lots, and buildings. Acceptance depends on review of the property, title, and associated obligations.

Household goods

Usable furniture and working appliances. Send a description and photographs so we can confirm whether we can accept them.

Money and business gifts

Support the work with a financial contribution through our existing Zeffy donation form, or contact us about a business donation.

Ask about free pickup

Free pickup is available within our service area by arrangement. Tell us what you have, your location, its condition, and whether it needs collecting. We will confirm acceptance and pickup availability before you make arrangements.

Acknowledgment of your gift

We provide a written acknowledgment for accepted donations. For property donations, we describe what we received without assigning a value. Please consult your tax advisor about valuation and any forms or appraisal requirements.

Items we cannot accept

Please do not bring hazardous materials, CRT televisions or monitors, mattresses, contaminated items, or items damaged beyond repair. Contact us first about any property with liens, title issues, or unpaid taxes.

How donated items help

Accepted items may be used in our programs, refurbished, sold to support the work, or recycled as appropriate.
