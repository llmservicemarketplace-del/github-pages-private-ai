When the insurance estimate doesn't match the restoration scope

Restoration contractors often work with multiple documents: carrier estimates, contractor estimates, photographs, measurements, invoices, and supporting notes. Keeping those records organized can make discussions about scope differences easier to follow.

PAi Claims provides a claim-documentation workspace for reviewing information and preparing supporting materials. It is not an automated contractor estimator or a guarantee of insurance payment.

Example workflow: reviewing a storm-damage claim

  1. Collect the insurance estimate and contractor's repair scope.
  2. Organize photographs, measurements, invoices, and other available evidence.
  3. Review the scope and compare relevant line items.
  4. Document differences, missing items, and questions in a worksheet.
  5. Prepare supporting information for the appropriate claim participants to review.

Frequently asked questions

Does PAi Claims automatically calculate a contractor's estimate?

No. PAi Claims is presented here as a documentation and worksheet tool, not as a replacement for estimating software or professional judgment.

Can contractors use it to review insurance estimate differences?

Its claim-documentation workflow can help organize information for comparing estimates and recording questions about scope or line items.

Does using the software guarantee a higher insurance settlement?

No. Claim decisions depend on policy terms, evidence, applicable law, and the insurer's review.

Does PAi Claims replace a licensed public adjuster?

No. Contractors should follow applicable state rules regarding insurance claim negotiation and public adjusting.
