Getting Started

  1. Download the PAi Windows installer.
  2. Open PrivateAI_Loader.exe.
  3. Enter the activation code from your purchase email.
  4. Allow PAi to analyze your computer.
  5. Choose from the compatible AI options shown by the installer.
  6. PAi will download and configure the selected AI automatically.

Do not download AI model files manually. PAi manages model downloads, hardware compatibility, installation, and configuration for you.
